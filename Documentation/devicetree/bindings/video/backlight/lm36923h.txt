TI LM36923H backlight controller on RED Hydrogen One

Required properties:
- compatible: must be "ti,lm36923h"
- reg: I2C address of the primary controller; must be 0x36
- hwen-gpio: GPIO driving the active-high hardware-enable input

Optional properties:
- current-limitation: maximum LED current in mA, in the range 1 through 25
- dual-3d-bkl: the RED dual-backlight assembly is fitted. The driver mirrors
  initialization, enable and brightness writes to the stock secondary address
  0x37. Probe fails if either controller cannot be initialized.

Example:

	lm36923h@36 {
		compatible = "ti,lm36923h";
		reg = <0x36>;
		hwen-gpio = <&tlmm 128 0>;
		current-limitation = <14>;
		dual-3d-bkl;
	};
